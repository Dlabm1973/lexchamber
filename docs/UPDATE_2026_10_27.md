# Seven-day trial and fees register

Free trials are seven days from backend activation. The access check caps a trial at seven days from its original start, including trials previously recorded as 30 days. Reconnecting does not restart the trial. Paid subscriptions are unchanged.

Pro Reports now provides an invoice fees register selected by invoice date (inclusive). It shows invoice number/date, client, matter name/ID, total invoiced fees, allocated payments received and outstanding balance. CSV and landscape PDF exports use the same model as the screen. Payment and outstanding columns show the current recorded position, not a historical reconstruction at the range end.

Invoice totals include VAT and billed disbursements where applicable. Drafts, invoices awaiting PDF issue, cancelled invoices and opening-balance migrations are excluded. Credits are identified separately. Missing historical receipts are flagged, never invented, and totals show known amounts. Invalid monetary values and undatable records are flagged for review.

This change does not need a Google Apps Script update. Verification covers inclusive dates, partial payments, credits, reversed/unallocated receipts, unverified historical records, long PDF rows, repeated headers, totals and trial boundaries.
