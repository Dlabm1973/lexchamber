BEFORE YOU BEGIN

A few clear
answers.

Can my secretary use LexChamber?

Yes. Your secretary registers and verifies their own email, then you link that account under Team access. They do not need to connect a backend. Secretary access covers firms, matters, draft invoices and payment recording. It excludes final invoice approval, reversals, banking and VAT settings, expenses, subscriptions and team administration.

Where are my practice records stored?

In the backend you connect and control. Google Sheets is currently supported. Authentication, hosting and delivery providers process information required to operate their services. Keep your own backups and review access permissions.

Can I bring records from another billing system?

Use the migration workspace to preview CSV files for firms, matters and unpaid opening balances. Import firms and matters first. Opening balances are carried forward without recreating tax invoices or historical receipts; verify the cutover date and reconcile totals before importing.

What happens after my trial?

Continue with LexChamber and arrange paid activation. There is no automatic charge. If access expires, your records remain in your own backend.

Is WhatsApp sending automatic?

No. LexChamber prepares your document and message for sharing. You review and confirm the recipient and attachment in WhatsApp. Email provider acceptance is also distinct from confirmed delivery.
