Conversation settings

Use a new conversation for each matter. The assistant follows your instruction naturally; these details guide its work.

Conversation documents

PDF, DOCX, TXT or Markdown · 8 files · 5 MB each · 100 PDF pages each. Check the extracted text before using it. Scans need OCR first.

Documents already used in a response are kept unchanged so their references stay consistent. Start a new conversation to replace them. No text is silently truncated.

Private pilot, clear limits.

Only your verified, paid account can use this workspace. Other subscribers and secretaries cannot open its APIs.

Use public, synthetic or properly anonymised material only, including filenames and voice recordings. Free Gemini may use inputs and outputs for product improvement and human review. OpenRouter requests require free, zero-retention endpoints with data collection denied. Availability may be limited; these settings do not guarantee confidentiality.

Documents are read in your browser. Sending a message transmits its text, the current conversation and all attached extracted text through LexChamber to the selected provider(s). Auto mode permits Gemini first, then OpenRouter on a capacity failure. No financial records are attached automatically.

Voice recordings stay in this tab until you choose Transcribe. Transcription sends the clip to Gemini only. Review and edit the transcript in the message box before sending it to the assistant. This is voice input, not a live voice call; use your phone keyboard dictation if recording is unavailable.

Conversations, documents and audio are held in tab memory. They are not intentionally stored in browser storage, Google Sheets or application logs. Export before closing or reloading. Clearing a conversation cannot remove records held by a provider. Provider and hosting processing remains subject to their own terms.

No live legal search, independent authority verification, OCR, image analysis, emailing or filing is available. Check every factual reference, admission, authority, time limit and prayer.
