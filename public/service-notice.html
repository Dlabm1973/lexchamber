Service, saving and delivery notices

Pending is not saved

Save pending means the change is held on this device awaiting confirmation from the backend. You may work on other records, but keep LexChamber open until confirmation. Never treat an optimistic screen update as a final posted ledger entry.

Uncertain outcomes and retries

A timeout can occur after Google has received a request. Use Retry same save rather than creating a fresh invoice or payment. Stable operation IDs protect against duplicate posting. Resolve conflicts against the underlying record before changing an uncertain submission.

Documents and recipients

Issued invoices require a final invoice number and a confirmed stored PDF. Check banking particulars, VAT and recipients. A WhatsApp text link cannot attach a local PDF; use Share PDF or download and attach it as a document. Provider acceptance, opening a share sheet and downloading a draft are not proof of delivery.

Reports and professional review

Reports use confirmed records and the stated date basis. Current balances are not automatically historical balances at a selected end date. Opening balances and unknown historical receipts require reconciliation. Outputs are working records for review, not audited financial statements.

Dependencies and backups

Google Apps Script execution, spreadsheet size, locks, quotas, network conditions and device limitations affect response times. Keep independent backups. Pending device-only work can be lost through storage clearing or eviction. Client control of the backend does not eliminate third-party processing or the need for security governance.
