Privacy and data handling

1. Who processes information

QAGA Data operates LexChamber. Contact info@lexchamber.co.za for privacy requests or suspected incidents. For account, support and subscription administration QAGA determines relevant processing purposes. A practice generally determines the purposes of its client records; to the extent QAGA processes them on its instructions, the responsible-party and operator arrangements must be recorded in the applicable written data-processing agreement.

2. Information and purposes

The service processes account identifiers, verified contact details, subscription and access information, practice connection settings, billing records, documents and operational request information as needed to authenticate users, enforce access, synchronise records, prepare documents, handle support and protect the service. Do not supply privileged material or unnecessary identity, health or financial details in support requests.

3. Storage and providers

Authoritative practice records are stored in the Google Sheets backend and document folder the practice connects and controls. Clerk processes authentication and relevant account metadata. The hosting provider processes service requests. Brevo receives recipient details, messages and PDF attachments when email delivery is requested. WhatsApp and the device’s share service process information you choose to share. Client-owned storage does not mean information never passes through service providers.

4. Browser storage and cookies

Authentication cookies or equivalent storage support sign-in. Pending changes and recent confirmed figures may be retained in browser IndexedDB or session storage for background saving and quicker reopening. This browser cache is not represented as application-encrypted. Protect your device and browser profile. Do not clear storage while work is pending. The app’s cache-clearing control is available only after pending saves are resolved. Clearing device data does not delete Google records or provider-held account information.

5. Access, security and cross-border processing

Access is limited through verified identities and practice roles; credentials must remain private. Security measures reduce risk but do not guarantee that every incident can be prevented. Providers may process information outside South Africa under their service arrangements. Applicable safeguards and any required agreements must be assessed for the deployment; no representation is made that all data remains in South Africa.

6. Retention and ending service

The practice controls retention and backups of its Google records and PDFs. Browser copies remain until removed or evicted and may be lost if browser storage is cleared. Account metadata, operational records and delivery records may be retained as reasonably required for service, security, legal obligations or disputes and under provider retention arrangements. No fixed automatic deletion period is promised by this notice. Request account closure or deletion by email; independently retained practice records and legally required records are dealt with separately.

7. Your requests and complaints

Subject to identity verification and applicable law, you may request access, correction or deletion, object to processing, or withdraw consent where consent is the applicable basis. A request cannot override another person’s rights or lawful retention duties. Contact info@lexchamber.co.za, identifying the request without disclosing secret keys. You may also complain to the Information Regulator at inforegulator.org.za. Practices remain responsible for handling requests concerning their own client records.

8. Incidents and processing instructions

Report suspected unauthorised access promptly to info@lexchamber.co.za. Where QAGA acts as an operator, applicable security incidents must be notified to the responsible party; statutory notifications to the Regulator and affected people must be handled according to POPIA. Processing on behalf of a practice requires the relevant written instructions, confidentiality and security arrangements. This notice does not replace that agreement or a PAIA manual where required.
