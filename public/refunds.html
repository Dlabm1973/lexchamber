Refunds, returns and cancellation

1. What we supply

LexChamber is an online practice-finance software service supplied by QAGA Data. We sell access to the software, not physical goods. Physical returns and shipping therefore do not apply. Access is provided electronically through the customer’s verified account after the required account and backend setup and, for paid access, confirmation of the agreed payment.

2. Free trial and payment

A seven-day free trial starts when the backend connection is activated. No automatic charge is made when the trial expires. The published subscription is R799 per month or R8,149.80 annually, paid upfront. All amounts are in South African rand (ZAR). Any applicable VAT and separately agreed setup or third-party charges must be confirmed in the quotation before payment.

3. How to cancel

Email info@lexchamber.co.za from your registered account email address, identifying the subscription and your requested cancellation date. Cancellation takes effect under your agreed subscription terms and any applicable statutory rights. We will confirm the effective date and any amount payable or refundable. Cancelling access does not transfer ownership of your practice records to QAGA; your underlying Google records remain in the backend you control.

4. Refund requests

To request a refund, email info@lexchamber.co.za with your account email, payment date, amount, payment or invoice reference and reason for the request. Explain whether the request concerns an incorrect or duplicate charge, inability to access the paid service, cancellation or another issue. Do not send card details, passwords or confidential client records.

We assess refund requests against the payment record, service supplied, agreed subscription terms and applicable law. There is no blanket no-refund rule. Eligibility, the amount refundable and any lawful reasonable cancellation charge depend on those circumstances. Starting to use the service does not remove rights that cannot lawfully be excluded. We will communicate the outcome and, if a refund is approved, the refund arrangements and expected processing time. Refund completion also depends on the relevant bank or payment provider.

5. Problems with the service

For access or technical problems, contact support@lexchamber.co.za or use the support form. Describe the problem and any displayed error so that we can investigate. This support process does not limit your cancellation, refund, complaint or other rights under the applicable agreement and law.

6. Contact and related policies

Customer support: support@lexchamber.co.za.

Cancellation, refund and billing requests: info@lexchamber.co.za or our enquiry form.

Read this policy with our Terms of Service and Privacy and data handling notice. Your applicable written subscription agreement and mandatory legal rights remain relevant. No purchase is completed through the website until payment facilities are enabled.
